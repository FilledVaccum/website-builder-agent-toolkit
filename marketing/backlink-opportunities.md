## Read this first

- **Most of the list is not worth your time.** A link from a site that accepts anyone adds little, and mass-submitting to low-quality directories can look like a link scheme to Google. See the [DRsubmission write-up](backlink-directory-submission.md), where a tool that submitted to 70 sites verified **0** live listings.
- **For AI assistants, the useful targets are editorial lists, reviews, communities and your own profiles** ([AI recommendation playbook, section 4](../seo/ai-recommendation-playbook.md#4-get-named-where-the-model-already-looks)). Bulk directories don't help there.
- **Data problems in the source list** (so don't trust it blindly):
  - Duplicates: Uneed, Dang AI, Quora, Google Business Profile and Entrepreneur each appear twice or more, and "Directory" and "Directory" appear as separate entries.
  - Test and dead entries: `Drtest`, `Exampledir`, `Dmoz` (shut down in 2017), "Bing Directory" and `HARO` (check whether it still runs).
  - Big publications listed as free guest posts (Forbes, Inc., WSJ, Wired, TechCrunch, Business Insider). These don't take free guest posts; several are marked Paid elsewhere in the list.
  - "Paid" entries sit inside a list advertised as "701 free". Skip any paid link meant to improve rankings.
  - Most links are **nofollow** or need editorial approval, so none of them guarantee a ranking.

## Tier 1: worth doing (real value, relevant to a new product or site)

These are real platforms where a profile or listing is normal and useful. Do them **by hand**, once, with accurate information.

| Goal | Targets from the list |
|---|---|
| **Your own brand profiles** (also help AI/entity recognition) | LinkedIn Company Page, Google Business Profile (if you have a location), Crunchbase, X/Twitter, YouTube Channel, GitHub Profile README, Gravatar, Pinterest/Instagram if relevant |
| **Launch and product discovery (software/startups)** | Product Hunt, BetaList, Indie Hackers, Hacker News ("Show HN"), Fazier, DevHunt, Microlaunch, TinyLaunch, SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, Wellfound |
| **Review sites (if B2B software)** | G2, Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice, TrustRadius, GoodFirms, Sitejabber (consumer) |
| **AI-tool directories (if you're an AI product)** | There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, Toolify, Future Tools, OpenTools, ToolPilot, TopAI.tools |
| **Developer and community platforms** | dev.to, Hashnode/HackerNoon, Medium (own profile), Substack, Reddit (in relevant subreddits, with disclosure), Stack Overflow Developer Story |
| **Design showcases (if design-led)** | Dribbble, Behance, Land Book, Awwwards (the source marks it Paid), Uplabs |
| **Local business (if relevant)** | Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, Foursquare |

## Tier 2: situational (only if relevant and the site still looks active)

- **Topic communities and Q&A:** Quora, Growth Hackers, Product School, Mind the Product, Elpha, eCommerceFuel, BiggerPockets, FreeCodeCamp Forum, SitePoint Forums. Answer questions genuinely. Link only when it truly helps, and disclose that you're affiliated.
- **Real editorial publications** that accept contributions: Search Engine Journal, Search Engine Land, Smashing Magazine, DZone, Social Media Today, eLearning Industry, Geekflare, MakeUseOf. Each has its own contributor guidelines and bar, and pitching needs a specific, expert idea.
- **Startup media and newsletters:** EU-Startups, GeekWire, Starter Story, Indie Hackers stories. Earned through a real story, not a form.

## Tier 3: skip

- **Generic web directories** with names like `Directory6`, `Hotdirectory`, `Freeprwebdirectory`, `Allstatesusadirectory`, `Besttopdir`, `Smartseobacklink`, `Theseobacklink`, `Textlinkdirectory`. These are link farms.
- **Anything marked Paid** that exists to sell rankings (e.g. GuestPostLinks, LinkPublishers, Serpzilla, and the crypto/finance "paid guest post" sites).
- **Test, dead or duplicate** entries, and entries with a domain that isn't about you.
- **Profile sites with no connection to your niche** (e.g. MyAnimeList or SoundCloud for a SaaS).

## How to run it

1. Pick **5–10 Tier 1 targets** that match your product. Use a dedicated email address.
2. For each: open the site, check it's active and you can submit free, read the rules, and write a unique description (don't paste the same blurb everywhere).
3. Track in a sheet: site, date, URL submitted, link type (follow / nofollow), status, live URL.
4. Check back in 2–4 weeks and record which actually published.
5. Measure referrals in GA4 and indexing in Search Console. Do more of what produces visits or mentions.
