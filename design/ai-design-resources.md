# Design resources: inspiration, templates, assets and AI builders

Twelve links from a "if you design with AI, this is all you need" post, checked on 2026-10-23 (title, description, pricing pages). **godly.website redirects to recent.design**, so it's 11 distinct sites. Cards with full tags are in [`library/items/`](../library/items/); index in [library/INDEX.md](../library/INDEX.md).

> Most of these are **inspiration galleries or paid asset packs**, not AI tools. Only Modulify builds a site from a prompt, and Motion sells prompts for sections. Use them as *references the agent can be pointed at*, not as dependencies.

## How an agent should use these

1. **Reference, then build.** Collect 3–5 examples from a gallery (Recent, Landdding, Footer Design, 60fps) and describe what you like about each. Give the agent those descriptions or screenshots with `frontend-design` / `ui-ux-pro-max` ([catalog stage 3](../catalog/stages/03-ux-ui-design-design-systems.md)).
2. **Shippable assets only from sources that allow it.** Iconly and Font In Logo are *reference* libraries; their icons and fonts belong to someone else. For things you can ship, use Lucide/Iconify ([stage 4](../catalog/stages/04-assets.md)) and check each font's license.
3. **Check licences and prices before paying.** Wonderlist, Motion and Backgrounds Supply are paid. Read their terms for client work.

## Caveats

- **"Cheaper than Lovable"** (Modulify) is the post's claim; I didn't compare against Lovable's current price.
- **"1 prompt, 1 website"** (Motion) is not what the site sells. It sells individual sections.
- Prices were read off the live pages today and change; recheck before buying.
- 60fps, Footer Design, Recent and on.design: I couldn't confirm pricing or gating (marked `unknown`).
